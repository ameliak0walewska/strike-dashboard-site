Read this before trusting any of the above. Entry/exit option prices are QuantLib model prices computed from historical realized volatility — not real historical market-traded option prices, which aren't freely available. A real trade would also face bid-ask spread and liquidity. If accuracy anywhere on this page is above roughly 65–70%, the far more likely explanation is a bug (usually some form of future-data leakage into training) than a real edge — real, working quant models on this kind of horizon typically land in the low-to-mid 50s%, applied across many bets.
